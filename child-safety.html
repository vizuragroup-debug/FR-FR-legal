Child Safety Standards

FR-FR is committed to protecting children and maintaining a safe environment for all users. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) on our platform.

1. Minimum Age Requirement

FR-FR is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a user under 13 has created an account, we will take steps to delete that account and associated data.

2. Reporting Mechanisms

Users can report inappropriate content, including any suspected child safety violations, directly within the app. Each message in FR-FR includes a "Report message" option that allows users to flag content for review by our moderation team. Reports are reviewed promptly, and appropriate action is taken, including content removal and account suspension.

3. Content Moderation

FR-FR employs the following measures to prevent and address child safety concerns:

  • In-app reporting: Every anonymous message can be reported by the recipient for harassment, hate speech, sexual content, threats or violence, spam, or other concerns.
  • User blocking: Users can block senders to prevent further messages from reaching their inbox.
  • Content review: Our team reviews reported content and takes action against violators, including permanent account bans.

4. Compliance with Laws

FR-FR complies with all applicable child safety laws, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and applicable regional and national regulations. We cooperate with law enforcement agencies and report suspected child sexual abuse material (CSAM) to the appropriate authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).

5. Account Termination for Violations

Any user found to be engaging in child sexual abuse or exploitation will have their account permanently terminated immediately, and their information may be reported to law enforcement.
