Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

FR-FR ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights over your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information — username, display name, and handle you provide during signup.
  • Messages — anonymous messages sent to you through the app or our web form.
  • Usage data — general app activity such as screens visited and features used, collected to improve the app.
  • Device information — device type, operating system, and timezone, used to provide sender hints and improve app performance.
  • Referral source — the platform a message sender came from (e.g., a social media app), used only for the hints feature.

We do not collect your real name, phone number, email address (unless you provide it for support), or precise location.

2. Anonymous Messages

FR-FR is built on anonymity. We do not reveal the identity of message senders to recipients. The hints feature provides general metadata clues (such as device type or timezone) — not personal identifiers.

While senders are anonymous to recipients, we may retain metadata associated with messages for safety and abuse-prevention purposes.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and deliver the FR-FR service
  • To show hints about anonymous senders (general metadata only)
  • To monitor for abuse, harassment, or policy violations
  • To improve app performance and features
  • To respond to support requests

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services to help run FR-FR, including database hosting, analytics, and crash reporting. These services may process some of your data as part of their operation. All third parties we use are contractually required to protect your data and may not use it for their own purposes.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account exists. Messages are retained to deliver the service and may be deleted upon account deletion. Usage and analytics data is retained for a maximum of 12 months.

6. Children's Privacy

FR-FR is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

🗑️ How to Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your FR-FR account and all associated data at any time by emailing privacy@vizura.app with the subject line "Delete My Account" and your FR-FR handle. We will process your request within 30 days. Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, messages, and all associated data from our systems.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of FR-FR after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, contact us at:
